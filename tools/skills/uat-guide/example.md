# Announcements UAT Guide

Oct 30, 2026 · @Moxie

Announcements lets office admins post updates to their people in Orbit and by email, replacing the daily Team Portal slideshow. Try it on [staging](https://staging.moxieorbit.com); if you like it, we release it to production.

**Deadline:** we want UAT finished this week, so please send all feedback by Friday, Nov 6, 2026.

## What it does

- **Write**: bold, lists, links and up to 3 images. Pick a category: Info, General, Urgent or Action Required.
- **Who sees it**: everyone in the office, or only some people by team, role level, location, department or name. A sentence and a live count show exactly who.
- **How and when**: in Orbit, by email, or both. Publish now or at a set time; an optional stop time takes it down.
- **What employees see**: a banner or pop-up in Orbit, and an Announcements list in the sidebar with a red unread count. Action Required has a **Mark as done** button.
- **What admins see**: who has seen, opened, dismissed or done each one. Published posts can be edited, re-sent as "Updated", duplicated or retracted.

## Trying it

- **Where**: [Orbit staging](https://staging.moxieorbit.com) (staging.moxieorbit.com), signed in as an Arizona Operations admin, under Admin → **Announcements**. The employee view is **Announcements** in the sidebar.
- **Safe to experiment**: staging is a test copy, so nothing reaches real employees.
- **Emails**: staging doesn't send to people's own inboxes. I'll forward you the emails your announcements produce.
- **Scheduled posts**: tell me the time and I'll start them. In production they go out on their own.

## By design (not bugs)

- **Upload CSV** is greyed out. Bulk upload of pre-written announcements comes later.
- **No repeating posts yet.** For a weekly reminder, Duplicate last week's and schedule it.
- **Who sees it is fixed once published.** To reach a different group, post a new one.
- **Only Arizona Operations** has it at launch; other offices are switched on later.
- **Images**: up to 3 per post. Videos go in as links.
- **Each office sees only its own** announcements and results.
